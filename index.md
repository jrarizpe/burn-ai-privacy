# BURN AI – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 25, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

BURN AI is a personal fitness and wellness application designed to provide adaptive workout recommendations, recovery insights, and training plans.

BURN AI is currently intended for private, personal use.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the integrations authorized by the user, BURN AI may collect and process:

- WHOOP recovery scores, strain, sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, and workout activity.
- Withings weight and body composition measurements.
- Apple Health activity and hydration information.
- Workout history, exercise performance, sets, repetitions, and training preferences.
- User-provided fatigue, wellness, travel schedules, and equipment availability.
- Account information required for authentication.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information is used exclusively to:

- Generate personalized workout recommendations.
- Evaluate recovery and training readiness.
- Adapt training intensity and volume.
- Track fitness progress.
- Generate weekly training schedules.
- Provide personalized wellness insights and reminders.

## 4. Third-Party Services

BURN AI uses or plans to integrate the following services:

- WHOOP API – Recovery, sleep, strain, and activity data.
- Withings API – Weight and body composition data.
- Apple Health – Authorized health and activity information.
- Supabase – Authentication, database storage, and backend services.
- OpenAI API – AI-powered analysis and personalized recommendations.

Information is shared with these services only as necessary to provide authorized application functionality.

BURN AI does not sell personal or health information or use it for targeted advertising.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

BURN AI is designed to use authenticated access, restricted database permissions, encrypted network communications, and secure server-side credential storage.

Health information and authorization tokens are intended to be accessible only to authorized users and services.

No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

## 6. User Consent and Permissions

Third-party health integrations require user authorization.

Users may revoke access through the corresponding third-party service. Revoking access prevents future authorized data collection but does not automatically delete previously stored information.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information is retained as necessary to provide application functionality.

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting the application administrator.

## 8. Artificial Intelligence and Medical Disclaimer

BURN AI provides fitness and general wellness recommendations. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment.

AI-generated recommendations may be inaccurate and should not replace professional medical advice.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as application functionality and integrations evolve.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
